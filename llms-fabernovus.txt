# FaberNovus

> Technology company focused on truth in communication and intelligent conversation platforms.

## What is FaberNovus?

FaberNovus is a technology company building AI-powered tools that help people communicate more effectively. Our flagship product is ResuMoose, an AI-powered resume builder.

## Products

### ResuMoose (https://resumoose.com)
AI-powered resume builder that helps job seekers create professional, ATS-optimized resumes. Users enter their career history once, then generate tailored resumes for specific job opportunities.
