About ResuMoose

ResuMoose is an AI-powered resume building platform that helps job seekers create professional, ATS-optimized resumes quickly and efficiently. Our platform understands that creating a resume is fundamentally a translation exercise—translating your valuable experience into a format that resonates with hiring managers and passes through Applicant Tracking Systems.

We believe your story deserves to be told well. ResuMoose helps you articulate the impact you've already made—the projects you forgot about, the problems you solved, the value you created.

How ResuMoose Works

  1. Build Your Career Profile: Enter your complete career history once. Your experience lives in one place.
  2. Find an Opportunity: When you find a job posting, our AI analyzes it and identifies the most relevant pieces of your experience.
  3. Generate Tailored Resumes: Our AI creates a customized resume that tells your story in a way that matches the opportunity.
  4. Review and Refine: You maintain full control—review the AI's suggestions, make adjustments, and send.
  5. Export and Apply: Download your polished, ATS-optimized resume as a PDF and apply with confidence.

Key Features
